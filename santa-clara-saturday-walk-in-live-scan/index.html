Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-09).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
  4. Cash vs card. All six INCLUDE rows list Cash plus card options on DOJ. Certifix AD West, UPS #2762, and The Connector Fashion Lane flag that additional service fees may apply. The Connector Fashion Lane lists Cash + credit + debit only (no billing accounts / checks on DOJ). DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. UPS and postal retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-09-09). The statewide Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF is still labeled “As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.” Do not budget from it.
