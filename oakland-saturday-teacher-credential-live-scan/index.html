Short CTC context

California Education Code §§ 44340 & 44341 require fingerprint clearance from DOJ and FBI through the Commission for credentials, certificates, permits, and waivers issued by CTC. Clearance through other agencies, employers, or states does not exempt you (Fees and Fingerprinting).

A Certificate of Clearance is not a clear teaching credential. It does not authorize instruction or services in schools; its purpose is verification of a professional fitness review (CL-900).

CTC does not maintain its own Live Scan shop directory. It points applicants to the DOJ Public Live Scan Sites list. Any DOJ Live Scan site works for CTC when Form 41-LS ORI fields are correct — this page does not invent “CTC-certified” shop badges.

FAQ

Which form do I use for CTC / teacher credential Live Scan?

California residents use CTC Form 41-LS (Request for Live Scan Service), which includes BCIA 8016 privacy notices. Download the official PDF from CTC — Form 41-LS — and follow 41-LS Instructions. Do not invent an ORI on a blank DIY BCIA 8016.

Can I invent or look up the CTC ORI myself?

No. CTC’s 41-LS instructions say the ORI is the “CTC number included - do not change.” Pre-filled agency fields must stay as shown. Incorrect ORI/mail code can delay or misroute results. Never publish or guess a CTC ORI — use the official form only.

How much does CTC Live Scan cost?

There is no honest single Live Scan government-fee total. CTC’s fees table for California Resident / Live Scan is “Varies - pay LS Operator.” Rolling fees are the shop table above. DOJ/FBI processing: check the requesting agency — do not invent a package dollar. Separately, the CTC COC online application fee is $50.00 + $2.65 = $52.65 — that is a CTC application fee, not a Live Scan fee (CL-900).

Does a Certificate of Clearance let me teach?

No. CL-900: the COC is not the same as a clear teaching or services credential and does not authorize instruction or services in schools. Its purpose is verification that you completed a professional fitness review.

Do I email my Live Scan form to CTC?

No. CTC: do not email your form to the Commission — scans are sent directly to CTC electronically. Print three copies of 41-LS, take them to the Live Scan site, and retain a copy for your records (CL-900; Fees and Fingerprinting).

Where do results go / how do I check status?

Results go to CTC via DOJ, not to you. Keep your ATI. Check status with ATI + date of birth at applicantstatus.doj.ca.gov. Official ranges: 3–7 days (DOJ) on CTC’s fees table; CL-900 usually within 10 working days. Also check CTC Online for Commission receipt.
