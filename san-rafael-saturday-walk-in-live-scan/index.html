Live Scan in San Rafael

Live Scan fingerprinting in San Rafael, California: this page lists 3 DOJ-listed Live Scan sites with a San Rafael street address, with walk-in vs appointment, rolling fees, and other-day hours where the DOJ lists them. 2 are open Saturday for walk-ins (see Open Saturday below). It is not every DOJ site in Marin County; the rest are on the DOJ county list.

Who this is for: You have a California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from an employer, licensing board, school, or agency, and you need fingerprints in the city of San Rafael: on a weekday or a Saturday, with or without an appointment.

Not for: TSA PreCheck / IdentoGO (different vendor). Out-of-state ink cards unless the shop’s own page says they roll FD-258. I-9s or notary work. Marin County as a whole: shops in Novato, Mill Valley, Fairfax, Kentfield, or Tiburon are other localities on the DOJ county list. San Ramon, a different city with its own page. Mobile-only providers with no San Rafael street address on DOJ.

Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-10-09).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number. The The UPS Store #6407 DOJ page also says “Additional service fees may apply.”
  4. Cash vs card. Both San Rafael Saturday walk-in INCLUDE rows list Billing Accounts, Cash, Credit Cards, and Debit Cards on DOJ. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. Shipping / retail hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan hours are the DOJ columns.
  6. Walk-ins. The UPS Store #6407 and The UPS Store #2623 both list Walk-ins on Saturday, with no appointments listed for that day. Marin County Sheriff’s Office lists no Saturday (see skip list).
  7. Odd DOJ hours. The UPS Store #2623 lists Saturday opening at 10:00 am, half an hour earlier than its weekday 10:30 am open. Shown as published; confirm before you go.
  8. Mobile flag. The UPS Store #2623 lists Mobile Services Available on DOJ; The UPS Store #6407 does not. This page is for the San Rafael street addresses above. Mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-10-09). The statewide Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF is still labeled “As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.” Do not budget from it.

FAQ

Where can I get Live Scan in San Rafael?

This page lists 3 DOJ-listed Live Scan sites with a San Rafael, California street address. 2 take Saturday walk-ins: The UPS Store #6407 and The UPS Store #2623. Other-day hours come from each shop’s DOJ page where shown. This is not every DOJ site in Marin County; see the DOJ county list for the rest. Confirm hours and fees with the shop before you go. Do I need an appointment for Live Scan in San Rafael?

It depends on the shop and the day. On DOJ, The UPS Store #6407 takes walk-ins on weekdays, Saturday, and Sunday; The UPS Store #2623 takes walk-ins on weekdays and Saturday; Marin County Sheriff’s Office is appointment-only Monday to Thursday and lists no Saturday. Appointments may still jump the line. Confirm before you go.

Can I walk in Saturday in San Rafael without an appointment?

Yes for the 2 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page (San Rafael street addresses only): The UPS Store #6407 (8:00 am – 4:00 pm) and The UPS Store #2623 (10:00 am – 5:45 pm). Both list Walk-ins on Saturday on DOJ. Marin County Sheriff’s Office lists no Saturday hours on DOJ. Nobody was called.

What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. INCLUDE rolling fees on DOJ: $38.00 (The UPS Store #2623) and $40.00 (The UPS Store #6407). The The UPS Store #6407 DOJ page also says additional service fees may apply. Ask the shop before you go.

Cash or card?

Both San Rafael Saturday walk-in INCLUDE rows on this page list billing accounts, cash, credit cards, and debit cards on DOJ. The UPS Store #2623 also lists company checks. Confirm with the shop before you go.

Are store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. Shipping / retail hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. Example: The UPS Store #6407 lists Saturday Walk-ins 8:00 am – 4:00 pm on DOJ. Those are fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every retail service is open.

Why isn’t Novato / Mill Valley on this list?

This page is the city of San Rafael, California only, not Marin County. Novato, Mill Valley, Fairfax, Kentfield, and Tiburon are other localities on the DOJ Marin County list and are not listed here; see the DOJ county list for those (accessed 2026-10-09). Other nearby cities are other cities. This page is also not San Ramon, which has its own Live Scan page.

Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.
