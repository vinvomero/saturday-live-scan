Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-14).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
  4. Cash vs card. Every INCLUDE row lists Cash plus card options on DOJ. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. UPS / Certifix / Postal Annex retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.
  6. Mobile flag. Four of five INCLUDE rows list Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the Redwood City street addresses above — mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
  7. Same address, two DOJ nodes. Mishsharm dba The UPS Store #2766 and Certifix Live Scan dbw The UPS Store #2766 both list 570 El Camino Real #150 / Suite 150. Different phones, fees, and Saturday windows on DOJ. Confirm which operator is working before you go.
  8. One weekday-only skip. Certifix UPS #4133 (Woodside Plaza) has no Saturday on DOJ — leave it off your Saturday plan.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-09-14). The statewide Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF is still labeled “As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.” Do not budget from it.

FAQ

Can I walk in Saturday in Redwood City without an appointment?

Yes for the 5 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page (city of Redwood City only). “Walk-ins & appointments” means appointments may still jump the line. Pack and Mail Express and Certifix UPS #2766 are walk-in-only on Saturday (DOJ does not list appointments). Nobody was called.

What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. Rolling fees on this page range from $35.00 (Pack and Mail Express) to $50.00 (UPS #7463).

Cash or card?

Every INCLUDE row lists Cash plus card options on DOJ. Pack and Mail Express and UPS #7463 also list billing accounts; Pack and Mail Express adds cashier’s check, corporate accounts, and money order. Confirm with the shop before you go.

Are UPS / Certifix store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS and Certifix pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. Example: Mishsharm UPS #2766 lists Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments on DOJ (9:00 am – 4:30 pm); Certifix at the same address lists Saturday Walk-ins 10:30 am – 1:00 pm. Those are fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every retail service is open.

Why isn’t Daly City / South San Francisco / Pacifica on this list?

This page is the city of Redwood City only. Daly City and South San Francisco already have their own Saturday walk-in pages. San Bruno, Pacifica, Burlingame, San Mateo, Belmont, Foster City, Menlo Park, Millbrae, and other San Mateo County cities are other cities (accessed 2026-09-14).

Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.

Earliest Saturday open (tie at 9:00 am): Postal Annex #4018, Mishsharm UPS #2766, and UPS #7463. Latest close: Pack and Mail Express at 5:00 pm. Highest fee: UPS #7463 at $50.00.
