Live Scan in Alameda

Live Scan fingerprinting in Alameda: this page lists 8 DOJ-listed Live Scan sites with an Alameda street address, with walk-in vs appointment, rolling fees, and other-day hours where the DOJ lists them. 3 are open Saturday for walk-ins (see Open Saturday below). It is not every DOJ site in Alameda; the rest are on the DOJ county list.

Who this is for: You have a California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from an employer, licensing board, school, or agency, and you need fingerprints in the city of Alameda: on a weekday or a Saturday, with or without an appointment.

Not for: TSA PreCheck / IdentoGO (different vendor). Out-of-state ink cards unless the shop’s own page says they roll FD-258. I-9s or notary work. Sunday Live Scan across the county (see the Alameda County Sunday page). Mobile-only providers with no Alameda street address on DOJ.

Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-10-06).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
  4. Cash vs card. All three Alameda Saturday walk-in INCLUDE rows list Cash and Credit Cards. The UPS Store #0447 lists only Cash and Credit Cards on DOJ (no debit cards). The UPS Store #0447 and The UPS Store #5898 list no Billing Accounts on DOJ. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. Shipping / retail hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan hours are the DOJ columns.
  6. Walk-ins vs walk-ins & appointments. Certifix Live Scan dbw The UPS Store #0578 and The UPS Store #5898 list Walk-ins on Saturday. The UPS Store #0447 lists Walk-ins & Appointments, so appointments may still jump the line. Three other Alameda street rows are appointment-only on Saturday and two list no Saturday on DOJ (see skip list).
  7. Mobile flag. The UPS Store #0447 (an INCLUDE row) lists Mobile Services Available on DOJ; the other two INCLUDE rows do not. This page is for the Alameda street addresses above. Mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
  8. INCLUDE rolling fees. INCLUDE rolling fees on DOJ are $45.00 (The UPS Store #0447, which also notes that additional service fees may apply, and Certifix Live Scan dbw The UPS Store #0578) and $48.00 (The UPS Store #5898).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-10-06). The statewide Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF is still labeled “As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.” Do not budget from it.

Do I need an appointment for Live Scan in Alameda?

It depends on the shop and the day. On DOJ, 5 of the 8 Alameda street rows take weekday walk-ins (2 list Walk-ins, 3 list Walk-ins & Appointments) and 3 are appointment-only on weekdays. On Saturday, 2 list Walk-ins, 1 lists Walk-ins & Appointments, 3 are Appt. only (one of them listed as Weekends), and 2 list no Saturday. Appointments may still jump the line. Confirm before you go.

Can I walk in Saturday in Alameda without an appointment?

Yes for the 3 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page (city of Alameda street addresses only): The UPS Store #0447, Certifix Live Scan dbw The UPS Store #0578, and The UPS Store #5898. What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. INCLUDE rolling fees on DOJ: $45.00 (The UPS Store #0447 and Certifix Live Scan dbw The UPS Store #0578) and $48.00 (The UPS Store #5898). The UPS Store #0447 also notes on DOJ that additional service fees may apply.

Cash or card?

All three Alameda Saturday walk-in INCLUDE rows on this page list cash and credit cards. The UPS Store #0447: cash, credit. Certifix Live Scan dbw The UPS Store #0578: billing, cash, corporate accounts, credit, debit. The UPS Store #5898: cash, credit, debit. The UPS Store #0447 lists no debit cards on DOJ, and The UPS Store #0447 and The UPS Store #5898 list no Billing Accounts. Confirm with the shop before you go.

Are store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. Shipping / retail hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. Example: The UPS Store #5898 lists Saturday Walk-ins 10:00 am – 6:00 pm on DOJ. Those are fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every retail service is open.

Why isn’t Oakland / San Leandro on this list?

This page is the city of Alameda only, not Alameda County. Oakland, Berkeley, San Leandro, Hayward, Fremont, and other Alameda County localities are other cities and are not listed here; they already have their own Live Scan pages. Other nearby cities are other cities (accessed 2026-10-06).

Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.
