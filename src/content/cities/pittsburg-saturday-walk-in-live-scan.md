## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-10-07).
3. **Two fees.** (a) Rolling fee = the shop's price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** All three Pittsburg Saturday walk-in INCLUDE rows list Cash, Credit Cards, and Debit Cards on DOJ. SKSS Enterprises, Inc. dba The UPS Store # 5984 lists no Billing Accounts on DOJ. DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** Shipping / retail hours on a shop's own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan hours are the DOJ columns.
6. **Walk-ins vs walk-ins & appointments.** SKSS Enterprises, Inc. dba The UPS Store # 5984 lists Walk-ins on Saturday. The UPS Store #7269 and The UPS Store #1064 list Walk-ins & Appointments, so appointments may still jump the line. E.N. Associates and Pittsburg Police Department list no Saturday on DOJ (see skip list).
7. **Two DOJ entries for The UPS Store #7269.** DOJ lists The UPS Store #7269 twice with the same street and phone: one entry at $38.00 with ZIP 94565, the other at $44.99 with "Ste. B" and ZIP 94583. Hours match on both. Ask the shop which fee applies before you go.
8. **Mobile flag.** The UPS Store #7269 (an INCLUDE row) lists Mobile Services Available on both DOJ entries; the other two INCLUDE rows do not. This page is for the Pittsburg street addresses above. Mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-10-07). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled "As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change." Do not budget from it.
