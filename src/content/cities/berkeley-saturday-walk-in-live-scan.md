# Saturday walk-in Live Scan in Berkeley

**Who this is for:** You have a California *Request for Live Scan Service* form (BCIA 8016) from an employer, licensing board, school, or agency, and you need fingerprints **this Saturday** without an appointment.

**Not for:** TSA PreCheck / IdentoGO (different vendor). Out-of-state ink cards unless the shop’s own page says they roll FD-258. I-9s or notary work.

**Updated:** 2026-08-31  
**Query this page answers:** “Saturday walk-in Live Scan Berkeley”

## How this was verified

Rows below are **only** shops whose **Berkeley street address** is on the California DOJ Public Live Scan Sites list **and** whose DOJ hours include **Saturday** and **Walk-in(s)**.

- Primary: [CA DOJ Live Scan locations — Alameda County](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/locations) (accessed 2026-08-31)
- Per-location DOJ pages linked in each row (accessed 2026-08-31)
- Operator pages used only for gotchas and for fields DOJ left blank (phones). They never created a row.

**Nobody was called.** DOJ itself says to confirm hours and fees before you go. If a cell could not be verified, it is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-08-31).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop’s price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number. P.O. Pack’s DOJ row also says **additional service fees may apply**.
4. **Cash vs card.** Several shops are cash/card. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. A1’s own fingerprinting page conflicts with DOJ on hours and fee — use the DOJ column until proven otherwise.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-08-31). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **“As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.”** Do not budget from it.

## FAQ

### Can I walk in Saturday in Berkeley without an appointment?

Yes for the 5 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows. “Walk-in & appt” means appointments may still jump the line. P.O. Pack and A1 are walk-in-only on Saturday (DOJ does not list appointments). A1 is a public conflict on hours/fee — use the DOJ column (10:15 am – 1:45 pm, $28). Confirm hours before you go. Nobody was called.

### What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

### How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. P.O. Pack’s DOJ row also says additional service fees may apply.

### Cash or card?

It varies by shop. UPS #6706 and #6089 list cash/credit/debit on DOJ (no billing accounts for #6706). A1, Mail Boxes Plus, and P.O. Pack list billing accounts plus cash/card options; Mail Boxes Plus and P.O. Pack also list money order. DOJ says to check for payment restrictions. Confirm with the shop before you go.

### Are UPS store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. UPS #6706 does have Sunday Live Scan on DOJ (10:00 am – 3:00 pm); UPS #6089 does not.

### Why isn’t Berkeley Live Scan on this list?

It has no street address on the DOJ detail page, and Saturday is **appointment only** 9:00 am – 12:00 pm (accessed 2026-08-31). This page is Saturday walk-in only.

### Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.

### What’s the cheapest Saturday walk-in in Berkeley?

A1 Photo Lab lists the lowest rolling fee in this table at **$28.00**, with the **shortest** Saturday window (10:15 am – 1:45 pm). Operator page conflicts ($20 / longer hours) — treat DOJ as the source of truth until proven. Next cheapest on DOJ is P.O. Pack at **$34.75** (additional service fees may apply), walk-in-only Saturday 10:00 am – 5:00 pm. UPS #6706 opens earliest at 9:00 am but rolls at **$50.00**.
