## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-07).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop's price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** All five INCLUDE rows list Cash on DOJ. UPS #0099 and Vee's Tax flag that additional service fees may apply. Copymat and Certifix UPS #6547 list **no Billing Accounts** on DOJ. DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS and postal retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-09-07). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **"As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change."** Do not budget from it.

## FAQ

### Can I walk in Saturday in Walnut Creek without an appointment?

Yes for the 5 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page. "Walk-ins & appointments" means appointments may still jump the line. Copymat is walk-in-only on Saturday (DOJ does not list appointments). Nobody was called.

### What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

### How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. Rolling fees on this page range from $35.00 (UPS #0099 and Vee's Tax) to $68.00 (Certifix UPS #6547). UPS #2414 is $38.00; Copymat is $40.00.

### Cash or card?

All five INCLUDE rows list Cash on DOJ. UPS #0099 and Vee's Tax flag that additional service fees may apply. Copymat and Certifix UPS #6547 list no Billing Accounts on DOJ. Confirm with the shop before you go.

### Are UPS store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. UPS #0099 and UPS #2414 list Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments on DOJ (both 9:00 am – 5:00 pm). Certifix UPS #6547 lists Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments on DOJ (10:00 am – 3:30 pm). That is fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every UPS service is open.

### Why isn't Certifix #6225 / UPS #0863 / Fingerprints Background Check on this list?

Certifix UPS #6225 is Saturday appointment only. Fingerprints Background Check Services is Weekends appointment only (city-only address on DOJ). The UPS Store #0863 is weekday-only walk-ins on DOJ. This page is Saturday walk-in only (accessed 2026-09-07).

### Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.
