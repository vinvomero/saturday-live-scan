## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-22).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop's price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** Mail All Center and The UPS Store #1847 list **Cash** plus card / account options on DOJ. WellnessMart, MD lists **no Cash** on DOJ (billing / corporate / credit / debit only). DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS and retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.
6. **Walk-ins & appointments.** All three INCLUDE rows list **Walk-ins & Appointments** on Saturday — appointments may still jump the line.
7. **Mobile flag.** Mail All Center lists Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the Mountain View street address above — mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
8. **Additional service fees.** Mail All Center and The UPS Store #1847 flag that additional service fees may apply (DOJ). Confirm before you go.
9. **Split Saturday hours.** WellnessMart, MD lists two Saturday slots on DOJ (morning + afternoon with a lunch gap) — both are shown in the table.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-09-22). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **"As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change."** Do not budget from it.

## FAQ

### Can I walk in Saturday in Mountain View without an appointment?

Yes for the 3 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page (city of Mountain View only). All three list Walk-ins & Appointments on Saturday — appointments may still jump the line. Nobody was called.

### What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

### How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. INCLUDE rolling fees: $17.00 (Mail All Center), $31.00 (WellnessMart, MD), $35.00 (The UPS Store #1847). Mail All Center and UPS #1847 also flag that additional service fees may apply.

### Cash or card?

Mail All Center and The UPS Store #1847 list cash plus card / account options on DOJ. WellnessMart, MD lists **no Cash** on DOJ (billing accounts, corporate accounts, credit, debit only). Confirm with the shop before you go.

### Are UPS store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. Example: The UPS Store #1847 lists Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments 10:00 am – 3:30 pm on DOJ. Those are fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every retail service is open.

### Why isn't Sunnyvale / Santa Clara / San Jose / Palo Alto on this list?

This page is the **city of Mountain View** only. Sunnyvale, Santa Clara, and San Jose already have their own Saturday walk-in pages. Palo Alto, Los Altos, Cupertino, Milpitas, Campbell, Los Gatos, and other Santa Clara County cities are other cities (accessed 2026-09-22).

### Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.
