## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-11).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop's price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** All four INCLUDE rows list **Cash** plus card options on DOJ. Ship Daly City and Post Point Hub flag that additional service fees may apply. DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS and Certifix retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.
6. **Mobile flag.** Post Point Hub and The UPS Store #6096 list Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the Daly City street addresses above — mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
7. **Odd zip on DOJ.** Certifix Live Scan dbw The UPS Store #0966 lists postal code **94115** with locality **Daly City**. Keep the DOJ text; confirm the address before you go.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-09-11). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **"As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change."** Do not budget from it.

## FAQ

### Can I walk in Saturday in Daly City without an appointment?

Yes for the 4 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page (city of Daly City only). "Walk-ins & appointments" means appointments may still jump the line. Certifix UPS #0966 and Post Point Hub are walk-in-only on Saturday (DOJ does not list appointments). Nobody was called.

### What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

### How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. Rolling fees on this page range from $39.99 (UPS #6096) to $45.00 (Certifix UPS #0966 and Post Point Hub). Ship Daly City and Post Point Hub may charge additional service fees.

### Cash or card?

All four INCLUDE rows list Cash plus card options on DOJ. Ship Daly City and Post Point Hub may charge additional service fees. Confirm with the shop before you go. Daly City Police (skip table) is cash-only on DOJ and has no Saturday hours.

### Are UPS / Certifix store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS and Certifix pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. UPS #6096 lists Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments on DOJ (9:00 am – 5:00 pm). Certifix UPS #0966 lists Saturday Walk-ins on DOJ (10:00 am – 6:00 pm). Those are fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every retail service is open.

### Why isn't San Francisco / South San Francisco on this list?

This page is the **city of Daly City** only. San Francisco already has its own Saturday walk-in page. South San Francisco, San Bruno, Pacifica, Burlingame, Millbrae, San Mateo, Redwood City, and other San Mateo County cities are other cities (accessed 2026-09-11).

### Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.
