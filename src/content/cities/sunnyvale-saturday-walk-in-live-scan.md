## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-10).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop's price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** All three INCLUDE rows list **Cash** plus card options on DOJ. KR Services flags that additional service fees may apply. DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS and retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.
6. **Mobile flag.** All Service Center and KR Services list Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the Sunnyvale street addresses above — mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-09-10). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **"As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change."** Do not budget from it.

## FAQ

### Can I walk in Saturday in Sunnyvale without an appointment?

Yes for the 3 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page (city of Sunnyvale only). "Walk-ins & appointments" means appointments may still jump the line. KR Services and The UPS Store #0067 are walk-in-only on Saturday (DOJ does not list appointments). Nobody was called.

### What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

### How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. Rolling fees on this page range from $25.00 (KR Services) to $55.00 (UPS #0067).

### Cash or card?

All three INCLUDE rows list Cash plus card options on DOJ. KR Services may charge additional service fees. Confirm with the shop before you go.

### Are UPS store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. UPS #0067 lists Saturday Walk-ins on DOJ (10:00 am – 4:45 pm). That is fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every UPS service is open.

### Why isn't San Jose / Santa Clara on this list?

### Do I get the results?
