## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-30).
3. **Two fees.** (a) Rolling fee = the shop's price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** All four Union City DOJ rows on this page list Cash. Card options vary by shop (see Payment column). DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** Shipping / retail hours on a shop's own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan hours are the DOJ columns.
6. **Walk-ins vs walk-ins & appointments.** Naka Tax & Insurance Services LLC, The UPS Store #3145, and VanLang Immigration & Tax LLC list Walk-ins & Appointments on Saturday. Total Multi Services Inc is Saturday Appt. only on DOJ (skip for Saturday walk-in).
7. **Mobile flag.** Naka Tax & Insurance Services LLC, The UPS Store #3145, and Total Multi Services Inc list Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the Union City street addresses above — mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
8. **INCLUDE rolling fees.** INCLUDE rolling fees on DOJ are $30.00 (Naka Tax & Insurance Services LLC; VanLang Immigration & Tax LLC) and $35.00 (The UPS Store #3145).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-09-30). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled "As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change." Do not budget from it.
