## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-05).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop's price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** Most shops in this include table list cash plus card options on DOJ. Postal Annex #164 lists cash and debit but **no Credit Cards** on DOJ. Certifix at UPS #0075 lists cash, checks, credit, and debit but **no Billing Accounts**. DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS and postal retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-09-05). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **"As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change."** Do not budget from it.

## FAQ

### Can I walk in Saturday in Concord without an appointment?

Yes for the 5 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page. "Walk-ins & appointments" means appointments may still jump the line. Walk-in-only on Saturday (DOJ does not list appointments): A1 Photo Lab and Certifix Live Scan at The UPS Store #0075. Nobody was called.

### What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

### How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. Rolling fees on this page range from $28.00 (A1 Photo Lab and Postal Annex #164) to $43.00 (Certifix UPS #0075).

### Cash or card?

Most shops in this table list cash plus card options on DOJ. Postal Annex #164 lists cash and debit but no Credit Cards on DOJ. Certifix UPS #0075 lists cash, checks, credit, and debit but no Billing Accounts. DOJ says to check for payment restrictions. Confirm with the shop before you go.

### Are UPS store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. UPS #0854 also lists Sunday Walk-ins & Appointments on DOJ (10:30 am – 3:30 pm). UPS #7668 lists Sunday Walk-ins & Appointments on DOJ (11:00 am – 4:00 pm). Certifix at UPS #0075 lists Sunday Walk-ins on DOJ (11:00 am – 4:00 pm). That is fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every UPS service is open.

### Why isn't JoAnn Cosme / AlKaabi / Johanna Torres on this list?

JoAnn Cosme Tax & Notary is Saturday appointment only on DOJ. AlKaabi, Beta Identify, ERSIC, and Seek & Find are Saturday or Weekends appointment only (and DOJ lists city-only Concord with no street for those four). Legal Office of Johanna Torres PC is weekday-only on DOJ. This page is Saturday walk-in only (accessed 2026-09-05).

### Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.

### What's the cheapest / earliest Saturday walk-in in Concord?

Earliest Saturday open: The UPS Store #0854 at 8:30 am ($38.00; 4425-C Treat Blvd.). Latest Saturday close: The UPS Store #7668 at 5:00 pm ($38.00).
