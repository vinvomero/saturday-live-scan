## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-04).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop's price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** Every shop in this include table lists cash plus card options on DOJ. Certifix at Aria lists cash, checks, credit, and debit but **no Billing Accounts**. DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS and postal retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-09-04). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **"As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change."** Do not budget from it.

## FAQ

### Can I walk in Saturday in Fremont without an appointment?

Yes for the 6 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page. "Walk-ins & appointments" means appointments may still jump the line. Walk-in-only on Saturday (DOJ does not list appointments): Niche Business Cafe and Certifix Aria Printing and Shipping. Nobody was called.

### What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

### How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. Rolling fees on this page range from $20.00 (Print N' Parcel) to $45.00.

### Cash or card?

Every shop in this table lists cash plus card options on DOJ. Certifix Aria lists cash, checks, credit, and debit but no Billing Accounts. DOJ says to check for payment restrictions. Confirm with the shop before you go.

### Are UPS store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. UPS #1640 also lists Sunday appt-only Live Scan on DOJ (10:30 am – 2:30 pm). UPS #1805 lists Sunday Walk-ins & Appointments on DOJ (10:30 am – 2:30 pm). That is fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every UPS service is open.

### Why isn't California Immigration / Santosh / Fremont PD on this list?

California Immigration Services Inc. is Saturday appointment only on DOJ. Santosh Services Inc. is Weekends appointment only (and DOJ lists city-only Fremont, CA 94539 with no street). Fremont Police Department, Livescan Identity, Postal Annex #111, and Certifix UPS #6139 are weekday-only on DOJ. This page is Saturday walk-in only (accessed 2026-09-04).

### Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.

### What's the cheapest / earliest Saturday walk-in in Fremont?

Cheapest rolling fee and earliest Saturday open in this table: Print N' Parcel at $20.00, open 9:00 am – 2:00 pm (3654 Thornton Avenue; short window, closes 2:00 pm). Second-cheapest: Niche Business Cafe at $25.00 (walk-in-only Saturday; opens 10:00 am). Latest morning open: Certifix Aria at 11:00 am ($45.00; no Billing Accounts on DOJ).
