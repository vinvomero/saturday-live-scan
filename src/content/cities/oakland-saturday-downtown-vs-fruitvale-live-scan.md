1. **Downtown (2 shops).** LPG sits in Census tract 4031, which the city names **Downtown/Old Oakland**. Allscan sits in tract 4030 (**Chinatown**). The city says Chinatown is "an important part of Downtown Oakland." ZIP **94607** is also West Oakland, so LPG is downtown because of the tract name, not the ZIP alone. We are **not** claiming Allscan sits inside the DOSP polygon.
2. **On Fruitvale Avenue (1 shop).** Copy USA's DOJ address is **3423 Fruitvale Avenue**. That street name is the assignment. The city's tract name for that address is **Oakmore South**, not Fruitvale. City Fruitvale labels (cultural district, Carmen Flores rec center, Table A-1 Fruitvale tracts) are **94601**. This page does **not** say Copy USA is in the Fruitvale district.
3. **Elsewhere in Oakland (4 shops).** Piedmont Ave Central, Adams Point East, Montclair North, Woodminster. Listed so we do not hide Saturday walk-ins. **Not counted** in either comparison column.

The city's East Oakland Neighborhoods Initiative list (Melrose, Elmhurst, Sobrante Park, Brookfield, Stonehurst, Coliseum area) contains **none** of these 7 Saturday walk-in shops. We did not stretch "East Oakland" onto Woodminster or Oakmore.

## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-02).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop's price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** Certifix lists **$45 cash / $47 card**. Copy USA's DOJ row says additional service fees may apply (no dollar). Card surcharge $ at Copy USA is **UNVERIFIED**.
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS pages advertise shipping. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.

## FAQ

### How did you split downtown vs Fruitvale?

We did not draw a neighborhood line. Each shop keeps its DOJ street and ZIP. The U.S. Census geocoder returns a Census tract for that address. The City of Oakland Table A-1 name for that tract is the neighborhood label. Downtown = LPG in **Downtown/Old Oakland** plus Allscan in **Chinatown** (city: "an important part of Downtown Oakland"). The Fruitvale Avenue column is Copy USA because the DOJ street contains **Fruitvale**. Copy USA's city tract is **Oakmore South**. We do not call it the Fruitvale district.

### Which Saturday walk-in shops are downtown?

**Two**, accessed 2026-09-02: LPG Live Scan at 524 7th Street (city tract Downtown/Old Oakland) and Allscan at 409 13th Street, 6th Floor (Chinatown; city treats Chinatown as part of downtown). Piedmont Avenue, Grand Avenue, La Salle Avenue, and Redwood Road are **not** in this count.

### Is there a Fruitvale Saturday walk-in?

**One shop on Fruitvale Avenue:** Copy USA, 3423 Fruitvale Avenue, Saturday walk-in 10:00 am – 3:30 pm, rolling **$35.00**. That is a street name on the DOJ row, not a claim that the shop sits in the city's Fruitvale district (94601). Card surcharge $ is **UNVERIFIED**. DOJ says additional service fees may apply (no dollar).

### What about Piedmont, Grand Lake, Montclair, and Redwood Road?

They are Saturday walk-in on DOJ and they are listed under **elsewhere in Oakland**. City labels: Certifix @ UPS #3270 = **Piedmont Ave Central**; UPS #1821 = **Adams Point East**; UPS #0243 = **Montclair North**; UPS #7098 = **Woodminster**. None of those names are downtown or Fruitvale Avenue, so they are not in either comparison column.

### Cheapest / earliest Saturday walk-in downtown?

### Cheapest / earliest Saturday walk-in on Fruitvale Avenue?

### Are UPS store hours the fingerprint hours?
