# Saturday walk-in Live Scan in San Francisco

**Who this is for:** You have a California *Request for Live Scan Service* form (BCIA 8016) from an employer, licensing board, school, or agency, and you need fingerprints **this Saturday** without an appointment.

**Not for:** TSA PreCheck / IdentoGO (different vendor). Out-of-state ink cards unless the shop's own page says they roll FD-258. I-9s or notary work.

**Updated:** 2026-08-31  
**Query this page answers:** "Saturday walk-in Live Scan San Francisco"

## How this was verified

Rows below are **only** shops whose **San Francisco street address** is on the California DOJ Public Live Scan Sites list **and** whose dedicated DOJ hours include **Saturday** (or Weekends) and **Walk-in(s)**.

- Primary: [CA DOJ Live Scan locations — San Francisco County](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/locations?county=San%20Francisco) (accessed 2026-08-31)
- Per-location DOJ pages linked in each row (accessed 2026-08-31)
- Operator pages used only for gotchas and for fields DOJ left blank (phones). They never created a row.

**Nobody was called.** DOJ itself says to confirm hours and fees before you go. If a cell could not be verified, it is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-08-31).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop's price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number. Fillmore Postal's DOJ row also says **additional service fees may apply**.
4. **Cash vs card.** Most shops in this table list cash plus card. **Fillmore Postal does not list cash on DOJ** (Billing Accounts, Corporate Accounts, Credit Cards, Debit Cards only). DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. Certifix/UPS #0361's UPS locator is 404 and third-party listings claim the store is closed — DOJ still lists Saturday walk-in; confirm before you go.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-08-31). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **"As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change."** Do not budget from it.

## After you scan

## FAQ

### Can I walk in Saturday in San Francisco without an appointment?

Yes for the 13 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows. "Walk-in & appt" means appointments may still jump the line. Walk-in-only on Saturday (DOJ does not list appointments): Post Box Inc., UPS #0178, Post Point Hub Mission, UPS #5568, UPS #6260. Certifix/UPS #0361 is a public stale-listing risk (DOJ open vs UPS locator 404) — confirm before you go. Nobody was called.

### What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

### How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. Fillmore Postal's DOJ row also says additional service fees may apply.

### Cash or card?

Most shops in this table list cash plus card options on DOJ. Fillmore Postal does not list cash (Billing Accounts, Corporate Accounts, Credit Cards, Debit Cards only). AAA/UnionPost lists cash/card but not Billing Accounts. Several UPS rows also list money order. DOJ says to check for payment restrictions. Confirm with the shop before you go.

### Are UPS store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. UPS #6260's locator says the store is open 7 days — DOJ has no Sunday Live Scan for that row. Post Box Inc. does have Sunday Live Scan on DOJ (walk-ins 10:00 am – 2:00 pm).

### Why isn't UPS #5818 / ID Solutions / Certifix Mission on this list?

UPS #5818 (60 29th St) is Saturday appointment only. ID Solutions is Weekends appointment only with no clock times. Certifix at 1875 Mission and Certifix at 447 Sutter are weekday-only on DOJ. This page is Saturday walk-in only (accessed 2026-08-31).

### Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.

### What's the cheapest / earliest Saturday walk-in in San Francisco?

Post Box Inc. lists the lowest rolling fee in this table at $29.00, walk-in-only Saturday 9:00 am – 5:00 pm (also Sunday walk-ins 10:00 am – 2:00 pm on DOJ). Tied for earliest open with Certifix/UPS #0361 at 9:00 am — but #0361 is $30.00 and carries a closed-listing gotcha. Next cheapest clean rows: AAA/UnionPost at $30.00 (opens 11:00 am) and the UPS #0178 / #5037 / #5402 band at $35.00 (open 9:30 am).
