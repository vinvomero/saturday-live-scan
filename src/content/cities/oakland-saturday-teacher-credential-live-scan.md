## Go / no-go before you leave

ORI and related agency fields are **pre-filled** — [“CTC number included - do not change”](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/41-ls-instructions/). Never invent an ORI on a blank BCIA 8016.
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted ([DOJ Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants), accessed 2026-09-01).
3. **Fees — keep them separate.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop’s price in the table below (varies by location). (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = check your requesting agency / CTC guidance; **do not invent** a single Live Scan government-fee total. CTC’s own Live Scan fee cell is **“Varies - pay LS Operator.”** (c) Optional separate **CTC COC application fee** online: **$50.00 + $2.65 = $52.65** — this is a **CTC application fee**, **not** a Live Scan fee ([CL-900](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-900/)).
4. **Results go to CTC via DOJ**, not to you. Keep your ATI number.
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS / print-shop pages advertise shipping or notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.

## Short CTC context

California Education Code §§ 44340 & 44341 require fingerprint clearance from DOJ and FBI **through the Commission** for credentials, certificates, permits, and waivers issued by CTC. Clearance through other agencies, employers, or states does **not** exempt you ([Fees and Fingerprinting](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/fees-and-fingerprinting/)).

A **Certificate of Clearance is not a clear teaching credential**. It does **not** authorize instruction or services in schools; its purpose is verification of a professional fitness review ([CL-900](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-900/)).

CTC does **not** maintain its own Live Scan shop directory. It points applicants to the [DOJ Public Live Scan Sites](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/locations) list. Any DOJ Live Scan site works for CTC when Form **41-LS** ORI fields are correct — this page does **not** invent “CTC-certified” shop badges.

## FAQ

### Which form do I use for CTC / teacher credential Live Scan?

California residents use CTC **Form 41-LS** (Request for Live Scan Service), which includes BCIA 8016 privacy notices. Download the official PDF from CTC — [Form 41-LS](https://docs.ctc.ca.gov/document/download/29865) — and follow [41-LS Instructions](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/41-ls-instructions/). Do not invent an ORI on a blank DIY BCIA 8016.

### Can I invent or look up the CTC ORI myself?

**No.** CTC’s 41-LS instructions say the ORI is the **“CTC number included - do not change.”** Pre-filled agency fields must stay as shown. Incorrect ORI/mail code can delay or misroute results. Never publish or guess a CTC ORI — use the official form only.

### How much does CTC Live Scan cost?

There is **no honest single Live Scan government-fee total**. CTC’s fees table for California Resident / Live Scan is **“Varies - pay LS Operator.”** Rolling fees are the shop table above. DOJ/FBI processing: check the requesting agency — do not invent a package dollar. Separately, the **CTC COC online application** fee is **$50.00 + $2.65 = $52.65** — that is a **CTC application fee**, not a Live Scan fee ([CL-900](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-900/)).

### Which Oakland shops take Saturday walk-ins for a teaching credential?

All **7** Oakland Saturday walk-in shops on DOJ tonight (accessed 2026-09-01): LPG Live Scan, Certifix @ UPS #3270, Allscan, Copy USA, UPS #7098, UPS #1821, and UPS #0243. Any DOJ Live Scan site works when Form **41-LS** ORI is correct — this page does not invent CTC-certified shop badges. Nobody was called. Confirm before you go.

### Does a Certificate of Clearance let me teach?

**No.** CL-900: the COC is **not** the same as a clear teaching or services credential and **does not authorize** instruction or services in schools. Its purpose is verification that you completed a professional fitness review.

### Do I email my Live Scan form to CTC?

**No.** CTC: do not email your form to the Commission — scans are sent directly to CTC electronically. Print three copies of 41-LS, take them to the Live Scan site, and retain a copy for your records ([CL-900](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-900/); [Fees and Fingerprinting](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/fees-and-fingerprinting/)).

### Where do results go / how do I check status?

Results go to **CTC via DOJ**, not to you. Keep your **ATI**. Check status with ATI + date of birth at [applicantstatus.doj.ca.gov](https://applicantstatus.doj.ca.gov/). Official ranges: **3–7 days (DOJ)** on CTC’s fees table; CL-900 usually within **10 working days**. Also check CTC Online for Commission receipt.
