# Saturday walk-in Live Scan in Oakland

**Who this is for:** You have a California *Request for Live Scan Service* form (BCIA 8016) from an employer, licensing board, school, or agency, and you need fingerprints **this Saturday** without an appointment.

**Not for:** TSA PreCheck / IdentoGO (different vendor). Out-of-state ink cards unless the shop’s own page says they roll FD-258. I-9s or notary work.

**Updated:** 2026-08-29  
**Query this page answers:** “Saturday walk-in Live Scan Oakland”

## How this was verified

Rows below are **only** shops whose **Oakland street address** is on the California DOJ Public Live Scan Sites list **and** whose DOJ hours include **Saturday** and **Walk-in(s)**.

- Primary: [CA DOJ Live Scan locations — Alameda County](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/locations) (accessed 2026-08-29)
- Per-location DOJ pages linked in each row (accessed 2026-08-29)
- Operator pages used only for gotchas and for fields DOJ left blank (phones). They never created a row.

**Nobody was called.** DOJ itself says to confirm hours and fees before you go. If a cell could not be verified, it is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-08-29).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop’s price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
4. **Cash vs card.** Several shops are cash/card; some add a card surcharge (Certifix lists it). DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS pages advertise “open 7 days.” That is shipping. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-08-29). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **“As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.”** Do not budget from it.

## After you scan

Results go to the **requesting agency**, not to you (DOJ applicants page). Keep the yellow copy / ATI number. If prints reject, resubmission rules are the agency’s, not the shop’s.
