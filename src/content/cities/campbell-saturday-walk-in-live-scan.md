## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-26).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop's price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** Verify Group lists **Cash** plus card/check options on DOJ. WellnessMart lists **no Cash** on DOJ (billing / corporate / credit / debit only). DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** Shipping / retail hours on a shop's own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.
6. **Walk-ins & appointments.** Both INCLUDE rows list **Walk-ins & Appointments** on Saturday — appointments may still jump the line.
7. **Lunch gap.** WellnessMart lists two Saturday DOJ blocks (ends 12:15 pm, resumes 1:00 pm). Plan around the gap.
8. **Mobile flag.** Verify Group lists Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the Campbell street addresses above — mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
9. **Two INCLUDE rolling fees.** Cheapest INCLUDE is **$30.00** (Verify Group, Inc.); highest is **$31.00** (WellnessMart, MD).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-09-26). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **"As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change."** Do not budget from it.
