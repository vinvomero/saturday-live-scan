## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-09).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop's price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** All six INCLUDE rows list **Cash** plus card options on DOJ. Certifix AD West, UPS #2762, and The Connector Fashion Lane flag that additional service fees may apply. The Connector Fashion Lane lists Cash + credit + debit only (no billing accounts / checks on DOJ). DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS and postal retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-09-09). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **"As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change."** Do not budget from it.

## FAQ

### Can I walk in Saturday in Santa Clara without an appointment?

Yes for the 6 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page (city of Santa Clara only). "Walk-ins & appointments" means appointments may still jump the line. Certifix AD West Mail Center and The Connector Fashion Lane are walk-in-only on Saturday (DOJ does not list appointments). Nobody was called.

### What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

### How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. Rolling fees on this page range from $18.00 (Certifix AD West Mail Center) to $30.00 (UPS #6844 and The Connector Fashion Lane).

### Cash or card?

All six INCLUDE rows list Cash plus card options on DOJ. Certifix AD West, UPS #2762, and The Connector Fashion Lane may charge additional service fees. The Connector Fashion Lane lists Cash + credit + debit only (no billing accounts / checks on DOJ). Confirm with the shop before you go.

### Are UPS store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. UPS #2762 lists Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments on DOJ (9:00 am – 4:00 pm). UPS #6844 lists 9:00 am – 5:00 pm. UPS #4636 lists 10:00 am – 5:00 pm. That is fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every UPS service is open.

### Why isn't San Jose / Sunnyvale on this list?

### Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.
