## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-10-09).
3. **Two fees.** (a) Rolling fee = the shop's price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number. Five of the seven INCLUDE DOJ pages also say "Additional service fees may apply."
4. **Cash vs card.** All seven Santa Rosa Saturday walk-in INCLUDE rows list Cash, Credit Cards, and Debit Cards on DOJ. The UPS Store #4739 and The UPS Store 7577 list no Billing Accounts on DOJ. DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** Shipping / retail hours on a shop's own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan hours are the DOJ columns.
6. **Walk-ins vs walk-ins & appointments.** The UPS Store # 5804, The UPS Store #4739, and The UPS Store #6261 list Walk-ins on Saturday. The UPS Store #2189, Gil's Business Tax Services, INC., The UPS Store 7577, and Postal Plus, Inc. list Walk-ins & Appointments, so appointments may still jump the line. The UPS Store 7669 is Saturday Appt. only on DOJ (see skip list).
7. **Odd DOJ address.** DOJ's first address line for The UPS Store 7577 is the store name itself; the street, 711 STONY POINT RD. STE 7, is on its second line. Shown as published.
8. **Mobile flag.** The UPS Store #4739 and Postal Plus, Inc. (INCLUDE rows) list Mobile Services Available on DOJ; the other five INCLUDE rows do not. This page is for the Santa Rosa street addresses above. Mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-10-09). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled "As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change." Do not budget from it.

### What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.
