## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-10-05).
3. **Two fees.** (a) Rolling fee = the shop's price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** All three Fairfield Saturday walk-in INCLUDE rows list Cash. Specialty Tax lists only Cash, Cashier's Check, and Money Order on DOJ (no credit or debit cards). Specialty Tax and The UPS Store #3954 list no Billing Accounts on DOJ. DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** Shipping / retail hours on a shop's own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan hours are the DOJ columns.
6. **Walk-ins vs walk-ins & appointments.** Specialty Tax lists Walk-ins on Saturday. The UPS Store #2110 and The UPS Store #3954 list Walk-ins & Appointments, so appointments may still jump the line. Three other Fairfield street rows have no Saturday on DOJ (see skip list).
7. **Mobile flag.** AlacritousOne (a SKIP row) lists Mobile Services Available on DOJ. No INCLUDE row does. This page is for the Fairfield street addresses above. Mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
8. **INCLUDE rolling fees.** INCLUDE rolling fees on DOJ are $36.00 (The UPS Store #2110 and The UPS Store #3954) and $45.00 (Specialty Tax, which also notes that additional service fees may apply).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-10-05). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled "As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change." Do not budget from it.
