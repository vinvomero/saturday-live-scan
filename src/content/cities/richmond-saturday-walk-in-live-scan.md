## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-08).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop's price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** WellnessMart lists **credit and debit only** on DOJ — **no Cash**. UPS #4612 and UPS #7242 list Cash plus card options. UPS #7242 flags that additional service fees may apply. DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS and postal retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-09-08). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **"As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change."** Do not budget from it.

## FAQ

### Can I walk in Saturday in Richmond without an appointment?

Yes for the 3 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page. "Walk-ins & appointments" means appointments may still jump the line. UPS #7242 is walk-in-only on Saturday (DOJ does not list appointments). Nobody was called.

### What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

### How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. Rolling fees on this page range from $31.00 (WellnessMart, MD.) to $50.00 (UPS #7242). UPS #4612 is $35.00.

### Cash or card?

WellnessMart lists credit and debit only on DOJ — no Cash. UPS #4612 and UPS #7242 list cash plus card options on DOJ. UPS #7242 may charge additional service fees. Confirm with the shop before you go.

### Are UPS store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. UPS #7242 lists Saturday Walk-ins on DOJ (9:15 am – 4:30 pm). UPS #4612 lists Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments on DOJ (10:00 am – 5:00 pm). That is fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every UPS service is open.

### Why isn't Richmond Police / STTICKY FINGERS on this list?

Richmond Police Department is weekday appointment only (Tue / Wed / Thu). STTICKY FINGERS, LLC is Tuesday appointment only (city-only address on DOJ). This page is Saturday walk-in only (accessed 2026-09-08).

### Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.

Earliest Saturday open: The UPS Store #7242 at 9:15 am ($50.00; walk-in-only Saturday; additional service fees may apply). Latest close: The UPS Store #4612 at 5:00 pm ($35.00).
