## Go / no-go before you leave

1. **Form first.** The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: [Applicants](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/applicants).
2. **Unexpired photo ID.** Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-20).
3. **Two fees.** (a) **Rolling fee** = the shop's price in the table. (b) **DOJ/FBI processing** = extra, amount depends on *your* agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single "Live Scan costs $X" number.
4. **Cash vs card.** The INCLUDE row lists **Cash** plus card / account options on DOJ. DOJ: "check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only."
5. **Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours.** UPS retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.
6. **Walk-in-only Saturday.** The Millbrae INCLUDE row lists **Walk-ins** on Saturday (DOJ does not list appointments for that day). Lines can still form — arrive early if you can.
7. **Single INCLUDE rolling fee.** The one INCLUDE shop lists **$40.00** rolling on DOJ.
8. **Thin city inventory.** Only **1** Millbrae Saturday walk-in row on DOJ — do not assume a second shop.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists **state $32.00** and **federal $17.00** for that specific COR submission ([source](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/custodian), accessed 2026-09-20). The statewide [Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF](https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/fingerprints/forms/fees.pdf) is still labeled **"As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change."** Do not budget from it.

### What do I bring?
