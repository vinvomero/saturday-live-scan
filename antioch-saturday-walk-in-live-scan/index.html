Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-10-03).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
  4. Cash vs card. All four Antioch Saturday walk-in INCLUDE rows list Cash. Card and billing options vary by shop (see Payment column). The UPS Store #0281 and Postal Annex #144 list no Billing Accounts on DOJ. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. Shipping / retail hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan hours are the DOJ columns.
  6. Walk-ins vs walk-ins & appointments. The UPS Store #0281 and Postal Annex #144 list Walk-ins on Saturday. ADETRONICS and The UPS Store #7094 list Walk-ins & Appointments on Saturday. Seven other Antioch street rows are Saturday appointment-only or have no Saturday on DOJ (see skip list).
  7. Mobile flag. ADETRONICS, Postal Annex #144, and several SKIP rows list Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the Antioch street addresses above — mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
  8. INCLUDE rolling fees. INCLUDE rolling fees on DOJ are $25.00 (ADETRONICS), $38.00 (Postal Annex #144), and $40.00 (The UPS Store #0281 and The UPS Store #7094).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-10-03). The statewide Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF is still labeled “As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.” Do not budget from it.
