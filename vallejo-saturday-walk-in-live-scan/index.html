Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-10-04).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
  4. Cash vs card. All three Vallejo Saturday walk-in INCLUDE rows list Cash. Card and billing options vary by shop (see Payment column). The UPS Store #1523 lists no Billing Accounts on DOJ. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. Shipping / retail hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan hours are the DOJ columns.
  6. Walk-ins vs walk-ins & appointments. All three INCLUDE rows (The UPS Store #1129, The UPS Store #1523, Glen Cove Mailbox Center) list Walk-ins & Appointments on Saturday, so appointments may still jump the line. Three other Vallejo street rows are Saturday appointment-only or have no Saturday on DOJ (see skip list).
  7. Mobile flag. The UPS Store #1129, Glen Cove Mailbox Center, and several SKIP rows list Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the Vallejo street addresses above. Mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
  8. INCLUDE rolling fees. INCLUDE rolling fees on DOJ are $30.00 (The UPS Store #1129) and $35.00 (The UPS Store #1523 and Glen Cove Mailbox Center).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-10-04). The statewide Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF is still labeled “As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.” Do not budget from it.

What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. INCLUDE rolling fees on DOJ: $30.00 (The UPS Store #1129) and $35.00 (The UPS Store #1523 and Glen Cove Mailbox Center).

Cash or card?

The UPS Store #1523: cash, credit, debit. Glen Cove Mailbox Center: billing, cash, cashier’s check, checks, company checks, corporate, credit, debit, money order. The UPS Store #1523 lists no Billing Accounts on DOJ. Confirm with the shop before you go.

Are store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. Shipping / retail hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. Example: The UPS Store #1523 lists Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments 9:00 am – 5:00 pm on DOJ. Those are fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every retail service is open.
