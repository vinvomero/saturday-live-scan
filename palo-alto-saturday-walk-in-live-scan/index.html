Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-23).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
  4. Cash vs card. Both INCLUDE rows list Cash plus card / check options on DOJ. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. UPS and retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.
  6. Walk-ins vs walk-ins & appointments. Certifix Green Mail lists Walk-ins on Saturday (no appointments listed for that day). UPS #6105 lists Walk-ins & Appointments — appointments may still jump the line.
  7. Mobile flag. Certifix Green Mail lists Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the Palo Alto street address above — mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
  8. Two INCLUDE rolling fees. Cheapest INCLUDE is $35.00 (UPS #6105); highest is $40.00 (Certifix Green Mail).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-09-23). The statewide Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF is still labeled “As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.” Do not budget from it.

FAQ

Can I walk in Saturday in Palo Alto without an appointment?

Yes for the 2 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page (city of Palo Alto only): The UPS Store #6105 and Certifix Live Scan dbw Green Mail. UPS #6105 lists Walk-ins & Appointments on Saturday (appointments may still jump the line). Certifix Green Mail lists Walk-ins only on Saturday (no appointments listed for that day). Nobody was called.

What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. INCLUDE rolling fees: $35.00 (The UPS Store #6105) and $40.00 (Certifix Live Scan dbw Green Mail).

Cash or card?

Both INCLUDE rows list cash plus card / check options on DOJ. UPS Store #6105: billing accounts, cash, cashier’s check, company checks, corporate accounts, credit, debit, money order. Certifix Green Mail: cash, cashier’s check, checks, credit, debit, money order. Confirm with the shop before you go.

Are UPS store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. Example: The UPS Store #6105 lists Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments 10:00 am – 6:00 pm on DOJ. Those are fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every retail service is open.

Why isn’t Mountain View / Sunnyvale / Santa Clara / San Jose on this list?

This page is the city of Palo Alto only. Mountain View, Sunnyvale, Santa Clara, and San Jose already have their own Saturday walk-in pages. Los Altos, Los Altos Hills, Cupertino, Milpitas, Campbell, Los Gatos, and other Santa Clara County cities are other cities. Menlo Park was previously researched with zero Saturday walk-ins — no page (accessed 2026-09-23).

Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.
