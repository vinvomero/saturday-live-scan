Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-23).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
  4. Cash vs card. Both INCLUDE rows list Cash plus card / check options on DOJ. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. UPS and retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.
  6. Walk-ins vs walk-ins & appointments. Certifix Green Mail lists Walk-ins on Saturday (no appointments listed for that day). UPS #6105 lists Walk-ins & Appointments — appointments may still jump the line.
  7. Mobile flag. Certifix Green Mail lists Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the Palo Alto street address above — mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
  8. Two INCLUDE rolling fees. Cheapest INCLUDE is $35.00 (UPS #6105); highest is $40.00 (Certifix Green Mail).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-09-23). The statewide Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF is still labeled “As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.” Do not budget from it.

What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total.
