Live Scan in Santa Rosa

Live Scan fingerprinting in Santa Rosa, California: this page lists 13 DOJ-listed Live Scan sites with a Santa Rosa street address, with walk-in vs appointment, rolling fees, and other-day hours where the DOJ lists them. 7 are open Saturday for walk-ins (see Open Saturday below). It is not every DOJ site in Sonoma County; the rest are on the DOJ county list.

Who this is for: You have a California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from an employer, licensing board, school, or agency, and you need fingerprints in the city of Santa Rosa: on a weekday or a Saturday, with or without an appointment.

Not for: TSA PreCheck / IdentoGO (different vendor). Out-of-state ink cards unless the shop’s own page says they roll FD-258. I-9s or notary work. Sonoma County as a whole: shops in Windsor, Rohnert Park, Petaluma, Healdsburg, Sebastopol, or Sonoma are other localities on the DOJ county list. Santa Clara, a different city with its own page. Mobile-only providers with no Santa Rosa street address on DOJ.

Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-10-09).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number. Five of the seven INCLUDE DOJ pages also say “Additional service fees may apply.”
  4. Cash vs card. All seven Santa Rosa Saturday walk-in INCLUDE rows list Cash, Credit Cards, and Debit Cards on DOJ. The UPS Store #4739 and The UPS Store 7577 list no Billing Accounts on DOJ. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. Shipping / retail hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan hours are the DOJ columns.
  6. Walk-ins vs walk-ins & appointments. The UPS Store # 5804, The UPS Store #4739, and The UPS Store #6261 list Walk-ins on Saturday. The UPS Store #2189, Gil’s Business Tax Services, INC., The UPS Store 7577, and Postal Plus, Inc. list Walk-ins & Appointments, so appointments may still jump the line. The UPS Store 7669 is Saturday Appt. only on DOJ (see skip list).
  7. Odd DOJ address. DOJ’s first address line for The UPS Store 7577 is the store name itself; the street, 711 STONY POINT RD. STE 7, is on its second line. Shown as published.
  8. Mobile flag. The UPS Store #4739 and Postal Plus, Inc. (INCLUDE rows) list Mobile Services Available on DOJ; the other five INCLUDE rows do not. This page is for the Santa Rosa street addresses above. Mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-10-09). The statewide Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF is still labeled “As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.” Do not budget from it.

What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. INCLUDE rolling fees on DOJ: $30.00 (The UPS Store #2189), $35.00 (Gil’s Business Tax Services, INC. and Postal Plus, Inc.), $45.00 (The UPS Store 7577), $50.00 (The UPS Store # 5804 and The UPS Store #6261), and $60.00 (The UPS Store #4739). Five of the seven DOJ pages also say additional service fees may apply. Ask the shop before you go.

Cash or card?

All seven Santa Rosa Saturday walk-in INCLUDE rows on this page list cash, credit cards, and debit cards on DOJ. The UPS Store #4739 and The UPS Store 7577 list no Billing Accounts on DOJ; The UPS Store #4739 also lists checks and company checks. Postal Plus, Inc., The UPS Store # 5804, The UPS Store #6261, and The UPS Store 7577 list cashier’s check, and Postal Plus, Inc., The UPS Store # 5804, and The UPS Store #6261 also list money order. Confirm with the shop before you go.

Are store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. Shipping / retail hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. Example: The UPS Store #2189 lists Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments 9:00 am – 5:00 pm on DOJ. Those are fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every retail service is open.

Why isn’t Petaluma / Rohnert Park on this list?

This page is the city of Santa Rosa, California only, not Sonoma County. Windsor, Rohnert Park, Petaluma, Healdsburg, Sebastopol, and Sonoma are other localities on the DOJ Sonoma County list and are not listed here; see the DOJ county list for those (accessed 2026-10-09). Other nearby cities are other cities. This page is also not Santa Clara, which has its own Live Scan page.

Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.
