Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-08-31).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table (cash amount where known). (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
  4. Cash vs card. Every shop in this table lists Cash on DOJ tonight. Certifix @ UPS #3270 is the only verified dollar bump: $45 cash / $47 card (+ gov fees) on the Certifix location page. Copy USA surcharge dollars are UNVERIFIED on copyusa.biz; DOJ says “Additional service fees may apply” with no dollar amount — do not invent $37. Other rows list cash and card on DOJ with no verified $ card bump tonight. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-08-31). The statewide Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF is still labeled “As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.” Do not budget from it.
