Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-16).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
  4. Cash vs card. The INCLUDE row lists Cash plus card options on DOJ. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. UPS retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.
  6. Mobile flag. UPS #0294 lists Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the San Mateo street address above — mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
  7. Thin city list. Only 1 San Mateo Saturday walk-in on DOJ tonight. A+ Mailboxes & More is Saturday appointment-only ($40.00 rolling) — leave it off your walk-in plan.
  8. One skip in city. See “Do not go here” below.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-09-16). Can I walk in Saturday in San Mateo without an appointment?

What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. The INCLUDE row lists $45.00 rolling on DOJ. A+ Mailboxes & More’s $40.00 is Saturday appointment-only (skipped).

Cash or card?

The INCLUDE row lists Cash plus card options on DOJ (billing accounts, cash, cashier’s check, checks, credit, debit, money order). Confirm with the shop before you go.

Are UPS store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. Example: UPS #0294 lists Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments 11:00 am – 4:00 pm on DOJ. Those are fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every retail service is open.

Do I get the results?
