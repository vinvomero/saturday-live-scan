Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-24).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
  4. “Additional service fees may apply.” Both INCLUDE rows (and the SKIP row) carry this DOJ note. The $13.99 and $15.00 rolling fees are what DOJ lists — the out-the-door shop price is UNVERIFIED (nobody called). Ask before they roll.
  5. Cash vs card. Both INCLUDE rows list Cash plus card / check options on DOJ. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  6. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. Notary / shipping hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.
  7. Walk-ins & appointments. Both INCLUDE rows list Walk-ins & Appointments on Saturday (neither is walk-in-only). Appointments may still jump the line.
  8. Mobile flag. Both INCLUDE rows list Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the Milpitas street addresses above — mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
  9. Two INCLUDE rolling fees. Cheapest INCLUDE is $13.99 (Calaveras Notary Services); highest is $15.00 (Post N’ Parcel).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-09-24). The statewide Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF is still labeled “As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.” Do not budget from it.

FAQ

Can I walk in Saturday in Milpitas without an appointment?

Yes for the 2 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page (city of Milpitas only): Calaveras Notary Services and Post N’ Parcel. Both list Walk-ins & Appointments on Saturday, so appointments may still jump the line. Oclarit Insurance Agency is Saturday appointment-only on DOJ. Nobody was called.

What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. INCLUDE rolling fees on DOJ: $13.99 (Calaveras Notary Services) and $15.00 (Post N’ Parcel). Both also say “Additional service fees may apply” on DOJ, so ask for the out-the-door price before they roll.

Cash or card?

Both INCLUDE rows list cash plus card / check options on DOJ. Calaveras Notary Services: billing accounts, cash, cashier’s check, checks, company checks, corporate accounts, credit, debit, money order. Post N’ Parcel: billing accounts, cash, cashier’s check, company checks, corporate accounts, credit, debit, IIS escrow accounts, money order. Confirm with the shop before you go.

Are store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. Notary / shipping hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. Example: Post N’ Parcel lists Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments 10:00 am – 2:00 pm on DOJ. Those are fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every retail service is open.

Why isn’t San Jose / Fremont / Santa Clara / Sunnyvale on this list?

This page is the city of Milpitas only. San Jose, Fremont, Santa Clara, and Sunnyvale already have their own Saturday walk-in pages. Newark, Union City, Cupertino, Campbell, and other nearby cities are other cities (accessed 2026-09-24).

Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.
