Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-09-15).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
  4. Cash vs card. Every INCLUDE row lists Cash plus card options on DOJ. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. UPS / Certifix retail pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column.
  6. Mobile flag. Certifix UPS #2354 lists Mobile Services Available on DOJ. This page is for the Burlingame street address above — mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).
  7. Thin city list. Only 2 Burlingame Saturday walk-ins on DOJ tonight. Execushield is Saturday appointment-only ($29.00 rolling) — leave it off your walk-in plan. Burlingame PD is weekday appointment-only.
  8. Two skips in city. See “Do not go here” below.

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-09-15). FAQ

Can I walk in Saturday in Burlingame without an appointment?

Yes for the 2 DOJ Saturday walk-in rows on this page (city of Burlingame only): The UPS Store #0446 and Certifix UPS #2354. Both are walk-in-only on Saturday (DOJ does not list appointments). Execushield is Saturday appointment-only — not a walk-in. Nobody was called.

What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. Both INCLUDE rows list $40.00 rolling on DOJ. Execushield’s $29.00 is Saturday appointment-only (skipped).

Cash or card?

Both INCLUDE rows list Cash plus card options on DOJ. UPS #0446 also lists billing accounts, checks, company checks, credit, and debit. Certifix #2354 lists billing accounts, cash, checks, and credit (no debit on DOJ). Confirm with the shop before you go.

Are UPS / Certifix store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. UPS and Certifix pages advertise shipping / notary hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. Example: UPS #0446 lists Saturday Walk-ins 10:00 am – 4:30 pm on DOJ; Certifix #2354 lists Saturday Walk-ins 10:30 am – 4:30 pm. Those are fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every retail service is open.

Why isn’t Daly City / South San Francisco / Redwood City / Pacifica on this list?

This page is the city of Burlingame only. Daly City, South San Francisco, Redwood City, and San Francisco already have their own Saturday walk-in pages. San Bruno and Pacifica were previously researched with zero Saturday walk-ins — no pages. Belmont, Foster City, Menlo Park, Millbrae, San Mateo, and other San Mateo County cities are other cities (accessed 2026-09-15).

Do I get the results?

No. Results go to the requesting agency, not to you. Keep the yellow copy / ATI number.
