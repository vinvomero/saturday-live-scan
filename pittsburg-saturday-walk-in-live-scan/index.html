Go / no-go before you leave

  1. Form first. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected. DOJ: Applicants.
  2. Unexpired photo ID. Expired IDs are not accepted (DOJ applicants page, accessed 2026-10-07).
  3. Two fees. (a) Rolling fee = the shop’s price in the table. (b) DOJ/FBI processing = extra, amount depends on your agency. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single “Live Scan costs $X” number.
  4. Cash vs card. All three Pittsburg Saturday walk-in INCLUDE rows list Cash, Credit Cards, and Debit Cards on DOJ. SKSS Enterprises, Inc. dba The UPS Store # 5984 lists no Billing Accounts on DOJ. DOJ: “check for any restrictions on method of payment, such as cash or money order only.”
  5. Store hours ≠ fingerprint hours. Shipping / retail hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan hours are the DOJ columns.
  6. Walk-ins vs walk-ins & appointments. SKSS Enterprises, Inc. dba The UPS Store # 5984 lists Walk-ins on Saturday. The UPS Store #7269 and The UPS Store #1064 list Walk-ins & Appointments, so appointments may still jump the line. E.N. Associates and Pittsburg Police Department list no Saturday on DOJ (see skip list).
  7. Two DOJ entries for The UPS Store #7269. DOJ lists The UPS Store #7269 twice with the same street and phone: one entry at $38.00 with ZIP 94565, the other at $44.99 with “Ste. B” and ZIP 94583. Hours match on both. Ask the shop which fee applies before you go.
  8. Mobile flag. The UPS Store #7269 (an INCLUDE row) lists Mobile Services Available on both DOJ entries; the other two INCLUDE rows do not. This page is for the Pittsburg street addresses above. Mobile pricing was not verified (nobody called).

Official state / FBI processing examples (not your total; purpose codes differ): DOJ Custodian of Records page lists state $32.00 and federal $17.00 for that specific COR submission (source, accessed 2026-10-07). The statewide Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF is still labeled “As of August 17, 2012 — Subject to Change.” Do not budget from it.

What do I bring?

A California Request for Live Scan Service form (BCIA 8016) from the requesting agency, and unexpired photo ID. The requesting agency fills the ORI / OCA / job title. A blank 8016 from the internet is usually rejected.

How much does it cost?

The rolling fee in the table plus the agency DOJ/FBI processing fee. Some agencies put a billing number on the form so you only pay rolling. There is no honest single total. Do not budget from the 2012 Applicant Fingerprint Processing Fees PDF. INCLUDE rolling fees on DOJ: $40.00 (SKSS Enterprises, Inc. dba The UPS Store # 5984), $45.00 (The UPS Store #1064), and The UPS Store #7269, which DOJ lists twice at the same street and phone with two different fees, $38.00 and $44.99. Ask The UPS Store #7269 which fee applies before you go.

Cash or card?

Are store hours the fingerprint hours?

No. Shipping / retail hours on a shop’s own page are not Live Scan hours. Live Scan Saturday hours are the DOJ column. Example: The UPS Store #1064 lists Saturday Walk-ins & Appointments 9:00 am – 5:00 pm on DOJ. Those are fingerprint hours from DOJ, not a promise that every retail service is open.
